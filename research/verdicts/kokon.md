# Kokon

A desktop app that edits a photo from a typed instruction, on the user's own computer. You brush an area and say what to change. One installer sets up an open image model (about 12 GB) on Windows, macOS or Linux, and it needs a GPU with 8 GB of VRAM or more. Made by Gabriele Lozupone. Licence AGPL-3.0; also published as a Docker image.

## Verdict: decline

Decline (why: imagery is in a build only when the proposal names it (`core/services.md`, Not in a build's price), and the house's own posts need no generative photo edits; no row of `core/stack.md` runs a local GPU model, and 12 GB of models on Jamie's machine buys nothing a service line sells · 2026-10-28).

## What would change it

- A proposal that names image editing as a line, with private photos that must stay on the client's machine.
- Content work that needs photo edits often enough to be a habit.

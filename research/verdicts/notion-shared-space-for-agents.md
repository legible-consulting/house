# Notion as a shared space for humans and agents

An Instagram carousel by Dave de Céspedes (`notioncoach`) from a talk at The 305 Social in Miami. He says AI makes each person do more, but a team does not win more, so AI workflows do not stick. His answer is a shared Notion space for people and agents, built in three moves: knowledge (talk the meeting out), state (AI turns the meeting into a launch page with an owner per task), and skills (context and instructions that agents reuse).

## Verdict: watch

Watch (why: the three moves map onto ICM, which the house teaches in its workshops (`core/services.md`, Workshop): knowledge is the reference material, state is the folder that proves the stage, skills are the contract; a workshop client who runs on Notion will ask how the two compare, but the post gives only the outline and not the build, and the house does not run on Notion · 2026-10-31).

## What would change it

- A workshop or coaching client works in Notion. Then a slide that maps the three moves onto ICM folders is worth making.
- He publishes the full talk or a written guide with the build in steps.

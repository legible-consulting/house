# Claude Startups

Claude Startups is Anthropic's program for early-stage founders. Members get offers, tools, events and partner discounts and credits worth up to $45,000. Third-party reports of the 2026-10-06 expansion say an approved company gets a free year of Claude Team (five seats) and $1,000 in API credits; Anthropic's page does not state these figures. The page says the current cohort is full and it does not accept new applications.

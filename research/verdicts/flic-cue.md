# Flic Cue

Flic Cue is a voice button from Flic (Shortcut Labs, Stockholm), with a Cue app on the phone or computer. You hold the button and talk; the app sorts what you said into a note, a list, a calendar entry, a message or an email, or sends it to an agent. It connects to your own server with a webhook or an MCP server, and to Claude, OpenAI or Gemini with your own API key, billed per request. It comes to Kickstarter in November 2026; the page gives no price.

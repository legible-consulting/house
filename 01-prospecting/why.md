# Why — 01-prospecting

The why and the date behind each rule of this folder's files. No Inputs name this file.
Read it when you change a rule, not when you follow one. A rule without a why is deleted.

## CONTEXT.md

- Where the kit and a model file disagree, the model wins and the kit is fixed
  Why: one document in one language, and a model file carries why-lines and law the salesman does not act on · 2026-10-28.
- The desk refuses a `slug` that exists in the pool or in `clients/*/record.md`
  Why: one company has one row, and a client is not a prospect · 2026-10-19.
- Jamie does not read the handoff before it lands
  Why: a constrained and structured salesman action goes straight to `main`, and the pull request gated nothing that a revert does not undo · Jamie, 2026-10-29.
- It carries the desk's identity, and the account that owns `DESK_PAT` moves `main`
  Why: a fine-grained token acts as its owner, the desk has no bot account, and the message names the salesman · 2026-10-29.
- The desk writes nothing else for it
  Why: the folder is the record, and the commit is the one door into it · 2026-10-29.
- A lead without a folder gets the same commit, with no move
  Why: the answers are documents of the relationship, and the client folder is its one home · 2026-10-26.
- Before any write, the desk stops the handoff in words, and the row stays where it was, when: a `ready` field is empty; the slug exists in `clients/`; or a lead file …
  Why: a handoff is one commit or none · 2026-10-29.
- A second one fails in words: nothing is on `main`, the row stays at `ready for Jamie`, and the sourcer hands off again from there
  Why: two writers on `main` at one moment is rare, and one retry ends it · 2026-10-29.
- No price reaches a prospect through it
  Why: `core/services.md`, "Never public" · 2026-10-22.
- It lives here because it governs the outreach this stage produces
  Why: the lawful basis sits beside the activity it covers, so the limits are read where the pitch is written · 2026-10-26.
- The slug never changes after a link was sent
  Why: a sent link names the slug · 2026-10-25.
- `key:` is required on every question
  Why: the old grammar made the key from the heading. Three languages give three headings and three keys. The answers of one form must line up across languages · 2026-10-25.
- Their options are in the same order too
  Why: the desk maps an answer to a pool field by the option's position. The words change per language. The position does not · 2026-10-25.
- No question and no option shows a price, a band or the floor
  Why: a form is a public page, and `core/services.md` keeps these numbers off every page · 2026-10-26.
- The keys do not
  Why: the desk maps a lesson to a status and a touch by its key · 2026-10-28.
- The handoff moves the folder into `clients/<slug>/` (The handoff, above)
  Why: the row changes many times a day and the answers do not · 2026-10-25.

## cadence.md

- `sourced_by` in `prospect.md` says who
  Why: the sender owns the thread, and a thread with two senders confuses the prospect · 2026-10-19.
- The desk sends nothing
  Why: the boundary. Nothing reaches a client from the house. A salesman's own account is their own · 2026-10-19.
- LinkedIn is not a channel
  Why: Jamie's answer · 2026-10-19.
- Email and WhatsApp open after they engage
  Why: Lei 41/2004 lets a company be mailed on an opt-out basis and needs a natural person's prior consent; a call and a visit are not electronic communications · 2026-10-26.
- The first message says where we found them. The sender fills in where
  Why: Art 14 GDPR, data not obtained from the person: the source and the right to object are said in the first message · 2026-10-26.
- Before adding a row, search the pool for the company: a company with that reason on a `lost` row is not added again, by any salesman
  Why: Art 21(2) GDPR makes an objection to direct marketing absolute; a no that did not stick is the whole objection · 2026-10-26.
- The desk lists a row as due for `lost` on day 28. The salesman sets it
  Why: a status is a salesman's call, never the desk's · 2026-10-19.
- `lost` is final for the row
  Why: the reason is the record, and a reopened row loses it.

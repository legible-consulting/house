## What does your business do, and for whom?

- key: business
- type: textarea
- hint: Two or three sentences, the way you'd tell a friend.

## What eats your week?

- key: eats_the_week
- type: textarea
- hint: The task, the chasing, the copying between tools: whatever you'd most like to stop doing by hand.

## Which tools do you use today?

- key: tools_today
- type: textarea
- hint: Spreadsheets, WhatsApp, a booking system, an accounting app, paper: all of it counts.

## If this went well, what would "done" look like six months from now?

- key: done_looks_like
- type: select
- options: A page or site people find and act on | A tool my team and I use every day | A process that runs itself and tells me when it needs me | Not sure, that's what I want the look to tell me

## Where can I see what you have today?

- key: links
- type: textarea
- optional: yes
- hint: Your site, your socials, a listing: links, one per line.

## A five-minute walkthrough (optional, but the best thing you can send)

- key: walkthrough_link
- type: text
- optional: yes
- hint: A screen recording of you doing the thing that eats the week, narrated. A link (Loom, Drive, WeTransfer). I watch it once, locally; it goes nowhere else.

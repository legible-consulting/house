# Target profile — who the pool goes looking for

One home for who prospecting chases, what makes a good lead, and what ends one. The kit's
author reads this, and a salesman reads it in the kit, `kit.<language>.md`, lesson
`who-we-look-for` (2026-10-28). From Jamie's answers of 2026-10-19 (wave step E1).

## Two lines, weighted to SMEs

- **Local operating business.** A café, a venue, a practice, a shop. One owner. Sites and
  small apps. This line keeps the referral engine warm. It is the core, not the growth bet.
- **SME with a process done by hand.** A company that repeats a task by hand today that
  software or an assistant could carry. Bigger engagements, retainer-friendly, remote-friendly.
  This is the line outbound chases first (why: the growth bet is the AI and agent-workflow
  line, and the local line already arrives by referral · 2026-10-19).

The `line` field in `prospect.md` carries which one a prospect is.

## Countries and languages

Portugal, Belgium and the United Kingdom. The languages are the three the client records
already use: `pt-PT`, `fr-BE`, `en-GB`. A prospect in another country is fine for remote work
when they read one of the three. Every touch is in the prospect's language (why: the client
reads their own language, the boundary in `CLAUDE.md` · 2026-10-19).

## A good lead

Signals. The more of them a lead shows, the harder it is to say no:

- **Came by referral or introduction.** Someone vouched. Trust exists before the first call.
- **A real operating business.** Customers and revenue today. The build serves something that
  already works.
- **One decider who pays fast.** One person says yes. The deposit arrives without chasing.
- **A process done by hand today.** A repeated task a person does that software or an
  assistant could carry. The SME signal.

## What ends a lead

Either one ends it. The salesman sets `lost` with the reason:

- **Haggling before scoping.** Price pressure before the work is understood, or a budget
  below the floor in `core/services.md`.
- **Third contractor on a haunted project.** Two developers already left the same project.
  The haunting is the product.

An idea-stage venture with no customers yet, or a vision with no done-looks-like sentence, does
not end a lead on its own. Jamie decides those on the call (why: Jamie's answer · 2026-10-19).

# Digital Platform, Booking & Commission Partnership

*Prepared for Agorasim — Countryside Experiences & Events*

> **The headline:** I've cut the build price from **€5,900** to **€2,000** — a 66% reduction.
>
> In exchange, we move to a light, growth-aligned commission on bookings that leaves you earning
> **more per tour than you do today** — while replacing the third party that currently takes €40
> a booking.

## 1. Where we are

Diogo, Rita — thank you for the last few weeks of conversation. You've been clear and fair
throughout, and I've listened. The scope we discussed is genuinely valuable, but the **€5,900**
figure is more than the business should carry as an up-front cost right now. You told me we
could look at something on the commission side if I could bring the price down. This proposal
does exactly that.

The idea is simple: I lower the up-front fee dramatically, and instead of a third party skimming
every booking, **a small commission flows to the platform that built and runs your booking
engine**. Crucially, the numbers below are built so that you keep more of every tour than you do
under your current provider — this is not a cost added on top, it's a *cheaper* replacement for
one you already pay.

## 4. The commission model — tour bookings

Today a third party sits between you and your guests on a regular day booking. Here's what
actually happens to the money:

- The guest pays **€470**.
- The third party keeps **€40**.
- Agorasim receives **€430** — which is actually **€10 below** your own €440 price point.

So the current arrangement both overcharges the guest and shortchanges you. Because feature #3
puts booking and payment on *your own* Stripe-powered site, we cut that third party out entirely.

**A commission that scales with the booking**

No two tours are the same — price moves with **group size, whether it's private or small-group,
and which activities are added** (Tasco Galapito, Manzwine, Ramilo Wines, Olaria MZ). A flat fee
can't handle that: it would gouge a small two-person add-on and barely touch a large private
day. So instead of a fixed amount, the platform takes a **percentage of whatever the guest
actually pays** — it simply grows and shrinks with the booking.

| Commission rate | Minimum | Capped at |
|---|---|---|
| **4%** of the booking total | **€10** per booking (small add-ons) | **€50** per booking (large groups) |

At 4%, the commission is **well under half** the effective ~8.5% the current provider takes on a
day booking. The floor keeps tiny bookings viable after processing costs; the cap means a big
private group with several activities is never over-charged. Stripe's own processing (~1.5% +
€0.25) is the only other deduction.

**On your regular day booking, here's how that lands against today:**

| Regular day booking | Now (3rd party) | Proposed (Stripe) |
|---|---|---|
| Guest pays | €470 | €470 |
| Payment processing (Stripe) | included in fee | ≈ €7 |
| Commission taken | €40 (8.5%) | ≈ €19 (4%) |
| **Agorasim keeps** | **€430** | **≈ €444** |
| vs. your €440 target | −€10 | +€4 |

The guest pays exactly what they pay today, yet **you keep about €14 more per booking (≈€444 vs
€430)** and, for the first time, clear your own €440 price point — while the platform's cut is
less than half the old one.

**The same rule across every kind of booking**

Because it's a percentage, one rule covers the whole range — from a short two-person tasting to
a full private day with add-ons:

| Booking scenario | Guest pays | Commission | You keep* |
|---|---|---|---|
| Couple · one tasting add-on | €150 | **€10** | **≈ €138** |
| Small-group day · 2 guests | €470 | **≈ €19** | **≈ €444** |
| Private full day · family of 4 | €900 | **€36** | **≈ €850** |
| Private day + add-ons · group of 6 | €1,400 | **€50** | **≈ €1,329** |

*\*After the 4% commission and Stripe processing. €150 booking meets the €10 floor; €1,400
booking hits the €50 cap (an effective 3.6%).*

Set the guest prices however you like for group size and activities — the commission always
follows the final total, so the model never needs re-negotiating when your pricing changes. If
you'd ever rather pass savings to guests than take the extra margin, the room is there; my
recommendation is to hold your prices and simply keep more.

## 5. Car hire for weddings & events

Car hire is a different animal from a day tour: higher value, booked further ahead, and it needs
a date held with a deposit and a balance settled before the event. Feature #6 (custom payment
links) is built for exactly this. It follows the same **percentage** logic as tours — just at a
rate set for the extra coordination each event carries.

**How a booking flows**

- Deposit to reserve the date — **30% of the total**, taken via a custom Stripe payment link,
  non-refundable inside a set window.
- Balance due **14 days before** the event, collected by a second automatic payment link.
- Confirmations, reminders and the balance request go out automatically over email & SMS
  (feature #5).

**Commission on car hire**

A platform commission of **6% of the booking value**, with Stripe's processing passed through.
The higher rate than tours reflects the extra coordination, custom quoting and payment-link
handling each event needs — and it still leaves the overwhelming majority of a premium booking
with you.

| Example — wedding car hire | €800 booking | €1,500 booking |
|---|---|---|
| Guest pays | €800 | €1,500 |
| Platform commission (6%) | €48 | €90 |
| Stripe processing (≈1.5%) | ≈ €12 | ≈ €23 |
| **Agorasim keeps** | **≈ €740** | **≈ €1,387** |

## 6. Why this is fair to everyone

A commission only works long-term if it's honest. Here's the reasoning laid bare, plus the
safeguards that keep you in control.

**Aligned incentives**

By dropping €3,900 off the price, I'm effectively investing in Agorasim's growth. At roughly €20
commission on a typical day booking, it takes on the order of **200 bookings** before that
discount is recovered — which means **I only come out ahead if the platform actually brings you
more bookings**. The GEO site, the auto-blog and the social poster all exist to do precisely
that. Your success and mine are pointed the same way.

**Your safeguards**

- **Still cheaper than today.** Tour commission is 4% (≈€19 on a day booking) vs the €40 a third
  party takes now — and a percentage is fairer than their flat fee at every booking size.
- **No hidden fees.** The commission and Stripe's processing are the only deductions. No monthly
  platform charge, no lock-in subscription.
- **Buy-out option.** At any time you can end the commission by settling the difference back
  toward the original build price — if the business booms and you'd rather own it outright, that
  door stays open.
- **Reviewed at 24 months.** We revisit the rates together after two years and adjust to
  whatever the business looks like then.
- **You hold the keys.** It's your Stripe account, your domain, your data. The commission is a
  fee on the service, not a claim on your business.

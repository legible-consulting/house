Agorasim run guided classic-car tours of the Saloia region, west of Lisbon, and classic cars for weddings and events.

## The problem

They are two people with four classic cars, and they drive the tours themselves. A third party took their bookings and kept a fee on each one. The guest's details were the third party's, not theirs, so a guest who came once was hard to invite back.

Weddings and events were quoted by hand, and the deposit and the balance were chased by message. And the site had to be found by travellers who plan a day out from Lisbon, in Portuguese and in English. Nobody in a two-person business has a free afternoon for that.

## What I built

**A site in Portuguese and English, made to be found.** It is structured so that search engines and the chat assistants travellers now ask can read it and point to it.

**Booking and payment on their own site.** The guest picks a tour, a date and a time, and pays on the page. A guest who cancels up to 48 hours before gets the refund automatically.

**A quote flow for weddings and events.** Agorasim builds the quote in the admin. The couple pays a deposit to hold the date, and the balance follows before the event, with one reminder.

**The emails a guest expects.** The confirmation, a reminder the day before, and a thank-you with a link to leave a review.

**An admin in Portuguese, made for a phone.** Bookings, phone and cash bookings, the calendar of cars and drivers, and the quotes, in one place.

## What changed for them

The new site runs on a test address, where they try each change before it reaches a guest. It goes live on agorasim.pt when they approve it.

From then, a booking made on their site is theirs. The guest's details land in their own records, and no third party sits between them and the guest.

Built with Next.js, Neon Postgres, Stripe, Resend and Vercel.

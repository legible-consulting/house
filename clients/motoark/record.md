garages buy from yards and dismantlers with VIN fitment checks, payments, delivery and seller
payouts in one place. Introduced by John Davy (chairman), after which Sam Cherry (COO & CTO)
wrote on 2026-10-18 asking "how we could potentially work together" and sent the overview pack
(Drive: `MotoArk Overview Pack.pdf`). A retainer. Discovery call held 2026-10-21 and follow-up call
held 2026-10-23 (`research/profile.md`). On the follow-up Jamie pitched ICM and showed the desk and
a pull request live. The price said: €3,700 a month, month to month, or the three months as one
job at €3,500, €3,500 and €3,700 with half up front and half at the end. Sam compares candidates
and decides Mon 26 Oct. The proposal and the quote are drafted in

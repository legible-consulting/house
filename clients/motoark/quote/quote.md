## What it costs

### The retainer

| | A month |
|---|---|
| The three evaluation months, November 2026 to January 2027 | €3,500 |
| The standard retainer, from month 4 if we continue | €3,800 |
| The three evaluation months in total | €10,500 |

Prices are in euros and exclude VAT.

The evaluation months are at the lower rate because you commit to the block of three, and
because this is recurring work rather than a one-off build. From month 4 the standard rate
applies, against a written monthly scope.

### What the rate covers

- The scope in this proposal, month by month, as written at the start of each month.
- My time, and the AI sessions I run, model cost included. There is no hourly billing and no
  timesheet. You pay for the written scope and what lands in the repositories.
- The weekly planning call and the monthly checkpoint.

Not covered: hosting, third-party services and licences, which stay in MotoArk's name; the two
developers' pay; travel, if you ask me to come to Sharjah, agreed per trip.

### Payment

- Monthly, in advance. The first invoice on signature. Each later invoice on the first working
  day of the month.
- By bank transfer, due 14 days from the invoice date.
- Month 1 starts on the Monday after the first invoice is paid and I have access to the
  repositories, the design files and the environments.

### Ending

- The evaluation is three months. It ends on 31 January 2027 unless we both decide to
  continue at the month 3 checkpoint.
- Within it, and after it, either side may end with 30 days' written notice. The month in
  progress is not refunded.

### Ownership and access

- The work lands in MotoArk's repositories. It is MotoArk's once the month it was built in is
  paid.
- I keep my method, my templates, my guards and my tooling, and use them again.
- Your code, hosting and accounts stay in MotoArk's name. My access lasts while the retainer
  runs.
- I may name MotoArk in my portfolio after version two ships, with your consent.

### Validity

This proposal, and the price in it, hold until 5 November 2026. After that I set the number
again.

### The parties, law and language

MotoArk, as the legal entity you name, and Jamie Nisbet, sole trader, Mafra, Portugal.
Portuguese law applies, and the courts of Lisbon are competent. This English text is the one
that counts.

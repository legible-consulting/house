## What it costs

### The retainer

Two ways to engage for the three evaluation months, 9 November 2026 to 7 February 2027.
You choose one.

| | A month | The three months |
|---|---|---|
| **Month to month** | €3,700 | €11,100 |
| **The three months as one job**: months 1 and 2 at €3,500, month 3 at €3,700, half of the fee on signature and half at the end of month 3 | €3,500, €3,500, €3,700 | €10,700 |

From month 4, if we continue: €3,700 a month, month to month, against a written monthly scope.

Prices are in euros and exclude VAT.

The one-job structure is lower because it commits the block of three and brings half of the fee
forward.

### What the rate covers

- The scope in this proposal, month by month, as written at the start of each month.
- Forty to sixty hours of my own attention a month, and the AI sessions I run with them, model
  cost included. How I spend those hours is my call. There is no timesheet for them: what we
  measure is the written scope and what lands in the repositories. Hours do not carry over
  from one month to the next.
- Hours beyond sixty in a month are agreed with you first, and invoiced at €120 an hour,
  listed on the next invoice.
- The weekly planning call and the monthly checkpoint.

Not covered: hosting, third-party services and licences, which stay in MotoArk's name; the two
developers' pay; travel, if you ask me to come to Sharjah, agreed per trip.

### Payment

- Month to month: in advance. The first invoice on signature. Each later invoice on the first
  working day of the month.
- The three months as one job: €5,350 on signature, and €5,350 at the end of month 3, when the
  job ends.
- By bank transfer, due 14 days from the invoice date.
- Month 1 starts on the Monday after the first invoice is paid and I have access to the
  repositories, the design files and the environments.

### Ending

- The evaluation is three months. It ends on 7 February 2027 unless we both decide to
  continue at the month 3 checkpoint.
- Within it, and after it, either side may end with 30 days' written notice. The month in
  progress is not refunded.
- The lower rate in the one-job structure buys the block of three. If you end the job before
  month 3 is over, the €200 a month between €3,700 and €3,500 is invoiced for each month
  delivered at €3,500, with the last invoice. The half paid on signature is not refunded.

### Ownership and access

- The work lands in MotoArk's repositories. It is MotoArk's from the start.
- I keep my method, my templates, my guards and my tooling, and use them again.
- Your code, hosting and accounts stay in MotoArk's name. My access lasts while the retainer
  runs.
- I may name MotoArk and describe the work in my portfolio from the first day of month 1. You
  agree to this when you sign.

### Validity

This proposal, and the price in it, hold until 5 November 2026. After that I set the number
again.

MotoArk, as the legal entity you name, and Jamie Nisbet, sole trader, Mafra, Portugal.

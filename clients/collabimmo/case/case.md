Collabimmo handle commercial property transactions in Belgium.

## The problem

They were clear about what they did not want: another agency site with listings on it. They asked for one sober page in their brand colours, whose only job is to get a visitor to fill in the form and tell them as much as possible.

Their real prospecting does not happen on a website. It happens in WhatsApp groups, where an opportunity is mentioned, a lead first appears, and both are forgotten. A group is a conversation, not a pipeline.

## What I built

**A page that does one job.** It is built around the enquiry form, and nothing on it competes with the form.

**Enquiries worth reading.** The form is protected against automated spam. A submission arrives formatted and complete.

**A pipeline from their WhatsApp groups.** The system reads the messages in their prospecting groups and sorts each one: a new lead, a property to note, a follow-up owed. It proposes the next action and waits for a yes. Nothing is filed without the owner's agreement.

**One view of the business.** Leads, deals in progress, properties, contacts and the calendar, in one place.

## What changed for them

The page collects what they need to know before a first meeting. The pipeline is built. It reads their real groups when their WhatsApp provider is connected.

Built with Next.js, the Claude API and Resend.

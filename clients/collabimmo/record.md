# Collabimmo

A commercial property agency in Belgium. They asked for one page built around the enquiry
form, and Jamie also built a pipeline that reads their WhatsApp prospecting groups and a view
of leads, deals, properties, contacts and the calendar. The build ended before the house
existed. The folder was opened on 2026-10-26 from the case on jamienisbet.com
(`case/case.md`), and holds no proposal, no agreement and no handover pack. The pipeline is
built and reads their real groups when their WhatsApp provider is connected.

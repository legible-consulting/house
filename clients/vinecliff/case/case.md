Vine Cliff Vineyards is a 170-year-old country estate on Lake Erie, in Brocton, New York.

## The problem

The estate rents a farmhouse in two units, a carriage house and a barn, by the night, the week or the weekend, and it hosts the odd wedding and reunion. The farmhouse was on Airbnb and a second house on VRBO. Both take a fee, even on a guest who comes back or who found the estate on their own. Events came by phone and word of mouth. The domain had never had a site.

The owner did not want a site that takes anyone's booking automatically. For a wedding on a heritage estate, he wants to know who is coming and what they plan before he says yes.

## What I built

**A page for each space, with live availability.** The guest sees which dates are free and an estimated total as they choose them, and sends a request. A wedding or an event starts as an enquiry.

**The owner approves every stay.** He sees who the guest is and what they want, and approves or declines from his phone. Nothing is confirmed without his decision.

**A guest who always knows where they stand.** The guest gets an email at each step and a private page that shows the state of their booking.

**An admin the owner runs himself.** He blocks dates, keeps notes on guests, answers enquiries, and changes each space's text, rates and rules with no developer.

Nothing is paid online. The owner collects the deposit himself.

## What changed for them

The site goes live at the end of December 2026 on vinecliffvineyard.com, ahead of the 2027 season. From then, a guest who comes back books direct, and no rental site takes a fee on that stay.

Built with Next.js, Neon Postgres, Resend and Vercel.

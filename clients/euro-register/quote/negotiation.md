## What the client may say, and the answer

- "Can we pay everything after the week?" The deposit is already down to 15 %. It covers
  the flight and holds the dates. No further move.
- "Can the escompte apply to the balance too?" No. The escompte rewards early payment of the
  deposit; the balance is due 14 days after delivery.
- "Can more people join the afternoons?" The 20 hours are fixed. More people means fewer
  hours each; the schedule is set together before the week.
- "Can you build the first system for us?" That is a separate job, quoted apart.

## Open before the first invoice

- **VAT.** Training given on site may fall under article 53 of the VAT directive (taxed where
  it takes place, Belgium) rather than the article 44 reverse charge. Belgium's reverse
  charge for non-established suppliers should avoid a Belgian registration either way. The
  accountant confirms before the deposit invoice. The agreement says "hors TVA" and that
  the B2B rules between two member states apply, nothing more.
- **The invoice address.** The register says Rue Jean Verkruyts 60, 4681 Oupeye. The
  address Arnaud gave on 12 October differed slightly. The agreement uses the register's.
  Arnaud confirms which one the invoice carries (question on his list).
- **A1 certificate.** Request the PD A1 from Segurança Social for the week in Belgium.
  Limosa is not required for self-employed training work.
- **The escompte.** A mandatory mention on the invoice in Belgian and French practice. The
  accountant confirms how the invoicing tool shows it.

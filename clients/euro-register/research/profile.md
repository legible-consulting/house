## The tool they use: Mammouth

Mammouth (mammouth.ai, French) is a multi-model chat: GPT, Claude, Gemini, Mistral, Llama
and Grok for text, plus image models. It has file upload, "Projects" (a workspace with its
own instructions and uploaded files), one-click re-prompting across models, web search,
and desktop and mobile apps. Plans and the exact model list change often; the team's plan
and seat count are on Arnaud's list. For the week this means: the "consigne" of a stage is a
Project's instructions, its inputs are the Project's files, and the folder structure lives on
their shared drive, not inside the tool. No agent runs over their folders. The week teaches
the method at the chat, consigne and folder layers, with the person as the runner.

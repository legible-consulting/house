Boys To Men Retreat runs four days in Ericeira for boys aged 9 to 16: challenge, adventure, and a bit of growing up.

## The problem

The people the retreat must convince are parents. They are asked to trust strangers in another country with their child for four days.

The old site was built for a desktop, and parents read it on a phone, in the evening. It spoke only English, and the families come from the Netherlands, Portugal, France and Spain. That asks a lot of a parent who is half-deciding in a second language.

## What I built

**A site that works on a phone.** The information that earns trust comes first and is easy to read on a small screen: who runs the retreat, what happens each day, what is included.

**Five languages.** English, Dutch, Portuguese, French and Spanish. A visitor lands in their own language and does not look for a flag in a corner.

**An application in short steps.** A parent starts it without bracing themselves. A completed application arrives in the team's inbox, laid out and ready to act on.

**The original words, kept.** I redesigned how the site reads, not what it says.

## What changed for them

A parent who reads about the retreat on a phone gets the full picture in their own language and can apply in the same sitting, instead of meaning to come back later.

Built with Next.js, Resend and Vercel.

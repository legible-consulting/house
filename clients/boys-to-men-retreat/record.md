# Boys To Men Retreat

A four-day retreat in Ericeira for boys aged 9 to 16, with families from the Netherlands,
Portugal, France and Spain. They asked for their site rebuilt for phones, in five languages,
with an application in short steps. The build ended before the house existed. The folder was
opened on 2026-10-26 from the case on jamienisbet.com (`case/case.md`), and holds no proposal,
no agreement and no handover pack.

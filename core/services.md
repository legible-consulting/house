# Services — what we sell, what each line includes, what it costs

One home for the lines and their prices. A proposal quotes from this file and deviates by
name. Work that fits no line is a finding: the file grows, or the work is declined (why: a
catalogue that is not the one home for price becomes a habit of quoting from memory ·
2026-10-19).

## The lines

| Line | The client gets |
|---|---|
| **Site** | Their business findable and presentable: pages, a contact, booking or menu flow, their own domain, live and handed over. |
| **App** | A working tool with state: accounts, data, an admin view, the workflows their business runs on. |
| **Platform** | An app with several roles or several surfaces over shared services: a marketplace, a product with its admin, docs and support. |
| **Workshop** | One day, on site or remote, that teaches ICM: folders as the architecture, markdown contracts as the interface, a human gate at every boundary. The materials stay with them, and an attendance record they can file as evidence of AI-literacy measures (EU AI Act, Article 4). |
| **Coaching** | One-to-one hours in which each person applies ICM to their own work, and a workspace rendered for them from the blueprint. |
| **Retainer** | Ongoing work against a written monthly scope. Hosting and basic support ride inside it (`terms.md` § Hosting and support). Handover offers it as the last section of the handover pack (`08-handover/retainer-offer.md`) (why: the client decides before the first month without support · 2026-10-19). |

Every build (site, app, platform) runs through the build: brief and tickets in the repo, a preview
environment the client signs off before every release, live status they can read at any
time, and a handover pack at the end (`08-handover/CONTEXT.md`). A role (`kind: role` in a
client record) is a position in the client's company in which Jamie owns part of the
product, not a line. It is agreed per case. A retainer at any number of hours stays a
retainer; the role starts where the ownership starts (why: Remi AI · 2026-10-22).

## In every build

- Domain, DNS and HTTPS.
- A privacy page and a contact route that fit the data collected. Analytics or none, with
  consent handling.
- For anything with state: an error tracker and a fail-safe page, so basic support can exist.
- The repository rendered from the blueprint, carrying its own pipeline, guards
  and `CLAUDE.md`.

## Not in a build's price

- **Content writing**: page copy, product text, translations. The client brings it, or it
  is its own line in the proposal (why: Jamie's answer · 2026-10-19).

Brand and graphic design, photography and imagery, and SEO beyond clean structure, metadata
and speed are not excluded by rule. Each is in a build only when the proposal names it.

## Pricing

Prices are in EUR and exclude VAT. Every engagement is a fixed price or a monthly retainer.
Time and materials is not sold (why: a fixed price makes scope the conversation; the
regretted deals were the ones where the price moved and the scope did not · 2026-10-19).

| Line | Price | Unit | How the number is reached |
|---|---|---|---|
| Site | €1,000 – €2,500 | per build | From the band. Below it is a very small build, still at or above the floor. Above it means real integrations, named in the proposal. |
| App | not banded | per build | Honest hours × the anchor, checked against the last closed proposal. |
| Platform | not banded | per build | As app. |
| Workshop | €1,800 | per day | One day of Jamie, on site or remote, materials and attendance record inside. Travel is a flat cost line outside it (§ Training pricing). |
| Coaching | €120 | per hour | The anchor. Sold as a fixed block of hours, never open-ended (§ Training pricing). |
| Retainer | not banded | per month | Jamie's hours of attention a month × the anchor, less the term discount (§ Retainer pricing). |

- **Anchor: €120 per hour.** Internal. It turns hours into a fixed price or a monthly
  retainer, and it is the rate Jamie names on a call so that a discount reads as one. No
  engagement has closed at it yet (2026-10-22). It is never a billing basis, except retainer
  hours beyond the promise (§ Retainer pricing) and the coaching hour, which is priced at
  the anchor (§ Training pricing · 2026-10-25).
- **Floor: €500.** Nothing goes below it. Below the floor the answer is the smallest honest
  version at or above it, or a decline.
- **Two options in every proposal:** the smallest honest version that does the job, and the
  whole scope. Options are scope. A lower number takes a named piece of scope with it.
- **Never public:** the anchor, the floor, the bands and the term discounts. Each is said on
  a call or in a proposal, never on a page (why: a number on a page is a number to argue
  with before the work is scoped · 2026-10-19).
- **Deviations are decisions.** A price below band is written in the client's `quote/`
  folder with its reason. The next proposal reads it as precedent.

## Training pricing

The workshop day and the coaching hour were distilled on 2026-10-25 from the first training
engagement, Euro Register's offer of 2026-10-09 (`clients/euro-register/quote/negotiation.md`).
The offer, not the agreed price, is the source: it was built from the anchor and read against
the market, and the agreed price is a relationship discount that the agreement names as no
reference rate (why: a list read from a discount is a discount · 2026-10-25).

- **A day is a day.** The workshop day is one day of Jamie, on site or remote. How the day
  splits between the group session and one-to-one hours is the programme; the price does
  not move with it. The materials, the starter kit and the attendance record are inside.
  The day at the offer was 1,820 € ex travel: about 12.7 honest hours at the anchor and a
  300 € premium for the method and the AI-literacy evidence. The market read of 2026-10-09
  put in-company AI training at 1,500 to 2,000 € a day.
- **Travel is a cost line.** Flights and transfers go in the proposal as one flat amount
  outside the day price. It may sit below the floor: it is a cost, not a service. The hotel
  is the client's.
- **The coaching hour is the anchor.** It prices hours sold outside a workshop day: remote
  sessions, a block after a week. The proposal sells a fixed block of hours, never an open
  count. The workspace rendered from the blueprint is inside the block.
- **Precedent.** Euro Register: 1,820 € a day ex travel at the offer, 1,220 € agreed, a 32 %
  relationship discount written into the agreement as no precedent.

## Retainer pricing

A retainer is priced from Jamie's hours, not from a range (why: a retainer runs from five
hours a month to near full-time, and one range fits none of them · 2026-10-22).

- **The unit is Jamie's attention.** The hours count Jamie's own attention in the month.
  What he does with them (sessions, calls, review) is his discretion. The sessions' time is
  not counted.
- **The price.** Hours a month × the anchor, less the discount for the committed term. The
  floor holds on the discounted monthly price.
- **The term discount.** The longer the committed block, the larger the discount (why: a
  committed block is income Jamie can plan on · 2026-10-22). 25% is the maximum. A block
  that renews keeps its discount.

| Committed term | Discount |
|---|---|
| Month to month | 0% |
| 3 months | 15% |
| 6 months | 20% |
| 12 months | 25% |

- **What the client sees.** The proposal and the agreement promise the hours a month.
  Delivery and progress are measured by scope completion, never by hours worked. Hours do
  not roll over.
- **Beyond the promise.** A month that needs more than the promised hours is absorbed, or
  the extra hours are billed at the undiscounted anchor. Jamie decides per relationship, and
  the quote says which.
- **Leaving a block early.** The 30-day exit in `terms.md` holds inside a block. When the
  client ends inside it, the term discount on the months already billed is invoiced with the
  last invoice (`terms.md` § Ending an engagement).
- **Precedent.** No retainer has closed at the rule yet. Sustentus and MotoArk sit below it;
  each is recorded in its `quote/economics.yaml`.

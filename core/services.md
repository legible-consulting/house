# Services — what we sell, what each line includes, what it costs

One home for the lines and their prices. A proposal quotes from this file and deviates by
name. Work that fits no line is a finding: the file grows, or the work is declined (why: a
catalogue that is not the one home for price becomes a habit of quoting from memory ·
2026-10-19).

## The lines

| Line | The client gets | Tier (`lexicon.md`) |
|---|---|---|
| **Site** | Their business findable and presentable: pages, a contact, booking or menu flow, their own domain, live and handed over. | `site` |
| **App** | A working tool with state: accounts, data, an admin view, the workflows their business runs on. | `app` |
| **Platform** | An app with several roles or several surfaces over shared services: a marketplace, a product with its admin, docs and support. | `platform` |
| **Workshop** | One day, on site or remote, that teaches ICM: folders as the architecture, markdown contracts as the interface, a human gate at every boundary. The materials stay with them, and an attendance record they can file as evidence of AI-literacy measures (EU AI Act, Article 4). | — |
| **Coaching** | One-to-one hours in which each person applies ICM to their own work, and a workspace rendered for them from the blueprint. | `workspace` |
| **Retainer** | Ongoing work against a written monthly scope. Hosting and basic support ride inside it (`terms.md` § Hosting and support). D3 offers it as one section of the handover pack (why: the client decides before the first month without support · 2026-10-19). | — |

Every build (site, app, platform) runs through D2: brief and tickets in the repo, a preview
environment the client signs off before every release, live status they can read at any
time, and a handover pack at the end. A role (`kind: role` in a client record) is a
position in the client's company, not a line. It is agreed per case.

## In every build

- Domain, DNS and HTTPS.
- A privacy page and a contact route that fit the data collected. Analytics or none, with
  consent handling.
- For anything with state: an error tracker and a fail-safe page, so basic support can exist.
- The repository rendered from the blueprint at its tier, carrying its own pipeline, guards
  and `CLAUDE.md`.

## Not in a build's price

- **Content writing**: page copy, product text, translations. The client brings it, or it
  is its own line in the proposal (why: Jamie's answer · 2026-10-19).

Brand and graphic design, photography and imagery, and SEO beyond clean structure, metadata
and speed are not excluded by rule. Each is in a build only when the proposal names it.

## Pricing

Prices are in EUR and exclude VAT. Every engagement is a fixed price or a monthly retainer.
Time and materials is not sold (why: a fixed price makes scope the conversation; the
regretted deals were the ones where the price moved and the scope did not · 2026-10-19).

| Line | Price | Unit | How the number is reached |
|---|---|---|---|
| Site | €1,000 – €2,500 | per build | From the band. Below it is a very small build, still at or above the floor. Above it means real integrations, named in the proposal. |
| App | not banded | per build | Honest hours × the anchor, checked against the last closed proposal. |
| Platform | not banded | per build | As app. |
| Workshop | open | per day | No number yet (`wave.md`, Jamie decides). |
| Coaching | open | per hour | No number yet (`wave.md`, Jamie decides). |
| Retainer | open | per month | Against the written monthly scope. No range yet (`wave.md`, Jamie decides). |

- **Anchor: €120 per hour.** Internal. It turns hours into a fixed price and answers the
  rare client who asks for a rate. It is never a billing basis.
- **Floor: €500.** Nothing goes below it. Below the floor the answer is the smallest honest
  version at or above it, or a decline.
- **Two options in every proposal:** the smallest honest version that does the job, and the
  whole scope. Options are scope. A lower number takes a named piece of scope with it.
- **Never public:** the anchor, the floor, the bands and the retainer range. Each is said on
  a call or in a proposal, never on a page (why: a number on a page is a number to argue
  with before the work is scoped · 2026-10-19).
- **Deviations are decisions.** A price below band is written in the client's `quote/`
  folder with its reason. The next proposal reads it as precedent.

# Identity — who Legible Consulting is

One home for who we are: the person, the entity, the place, the languages, and what we do
and do not do. Every module and every client artefact reads these facts here (why: one home
per fact; a bio restated in five proposals drifts five ways · 2026-10-19).

## Bio

Jamie Nisbet is a software engineer and AI consultant in Mafra, Portugal. He runs Legible
Consulting, which builds sites, apps and platforms for clients and teaches the Interpretable
Context Methodology (ICM) in workshops and coaching. He is the one engineer on every build:
he takes the call, scopes the work at a fixed price, builds it, and hands over a repository
that carries its own way of working, so the client's team or any developer can carry on
without him. He has worked this way since 2021 for businesses in Portugal, Belgium, the UK,
the UAE and the United States.

## Languages

Jamie works with clients in English, French and Portuguese. English is the default and the
internal language. A client folder's `language:` field says which one that client reads.

## What we do

Two lines. The org front page restates them in public.

- **Build.** A client site, app or platform at a fixed price, through one pipeline: brief
  and tickets in the repo, a preview environment the client signs off before every release,
  live status they can read at any time, and a handover pack at the end.
- **Teach.** ICM in a one-day workshop, and coaching that leaves a client with their own
  working workspace.

The lines, what each includes and what each costs are in `services.md`.

## What we do not do

- Work with no build and no teaching in it: copywriting, branding or design as the
  deliverable itself. We point at someone who does it well.
- Time and materials. Every engagement is a fixed price or a written monthly scope
  (`services.md`).

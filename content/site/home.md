## hero

### I build the software your business runs on, and you own all of it at the end.

I'm Jamie, an engineer in Mafra, Portugal. I take the first call, I scope the work at a fixed price, I build it, and I hand it over. You deal with one person from the first message to the last release.

## whatIDo

### Three things: I build, I teach, and I advise.

- **I build.** A site, an app or a larger system for your business, at a fixed price. You see every change on a preview and say yes before it goes live. You can read where the work stands at any time. At the end you get a handover pack.
- **I teach.** A one-day workshop in the Interpretable Context Methodology, the way I organise my own work: folders and short written contracts, so that a new colleague or an assistant can take the work from the files alone. Then coaching hours that leave you with a working setup of your own.
- **I advise.** A straight second opinion on where these tools fit in your business, or someone technical to keep a build on track.

What each one includes is on /services.

## howItWorks

### The same way, every time.

1. **You tell me how the business runs.** I tell you what is worth building, what is not, and what I won't know until I see more.
2. **A written proposal with two options.** The smallest honest version that does the job, and the whole scope. Each has a fixed price. A lower price takes a named piece of the scope with it.
3. **The build.** The plan and the tasks are in your own repository. Before every release, you see the change on a preview and you say yes.
4. **The handover.** Your domain, your accounts and your code, in a repository that carries its own way of working. Your team, or any developer, can carry on without me.

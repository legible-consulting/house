## services

### What I sell, and what each one includes.

Fourteen things, in four groups. Each one is a fixed price or a scope we write down for each month. The price is in the proposal, once I know what the job is.

## build

### I build it.

- **Site.** Your business, easy to find and good to look at: the pages, a way to contact you, to book or to see the menu, on your own domain. Live, and handed over to you.
- **Landing pages, fully serviced.** One page, designed, written, hosted and kept current by me. The words are part of the job, and you never chase anyone for a change.
- **App.** A working tool that keeps your data: accounts, an admin view, and the steps your business runs on every day.
- **Platform.** An app for several kinds of user, or several apps that share the same data: a marketplace, or a product with its admin, its docs and its support.
- **Software engineering.** Web apps and systems built to last: fast, accessible, documented, and easy to change.
- **AI & non-AI automation.** The repeated steps of your week, done by software. I connect your tools and automate the steps nobody wants to do, with a model where it earns its place and plain code where it doesn't.
- **AI business infrastructure.** The base that makes an assistant useful across your business: a knowledge base, search over your own documents, and the parts that keep its answers grounded in your data.
- **GEO optimisation.** Your business named in the answers that ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity give, not only in search results. I structure your content and data so that they cite you when your customers ask.
- **Lead-gen channel development.** The channels that bring work in: referrals, outreach, and the tools behind them, so that new work doesn't depend on word of mouth alone.

## teach

### I teach it.

- **Workshop.** One day, at your place or remote, that teaches the Interpretable Context Methodology: folders as the plan of the work, short written contracts that say what each step takes and gives, and a person who says yes at every handoff. You keep the materials, and an attendance record you can file under Article 4 of the EU AI Act.
- **Coaching.** Hours one to one, in which each person applies the method to their own work. Each person leaves with a workspace of their own, set up from my public template.

## advise

### I tell you what I'd do.

- **AI consultancy.** A straight second opinion on where these tools fit in your business, and where they don't. I find the few uses worth doing, and I name the rest so you can skip them.
- **Project management.** Someone technical who keeps a build on track: the scope, the timeline and the people on it, with honest status you can act on.

## retainer

### I stay on it.

- **Retainer.** Ongoing work against a scope we write down each month. Hosting and basic support are inside it.

## inEveryBuild

### In every build.

- Your domain, its DNS and HTTPS.
- A privacy page and a way to contact you that fit the data you collect. Analytics, or none, with consent handled.
- For anything that keeps data: an error tracker and a fallback page, so that support can exist.
- A repository with its own way of working inside it: its checks, its guards and a short guide for the next person.
- A preview you sign off before every release, live status you can read at any time, and a handover pack at the end.

## notInThePrice

### Not in a build's price.

- Writing the words: page text, product text, translations. You bring them, or they are their own line in the proposal. A fully serviced landing page is the one exception: its words are inside it.
- Brand and graphic design, photos and images, and search work beyond a clean structure, good metadata and speed. Each one is in a build only when the proposal names it.

## pricing

### How a price is set.

- Every job is a fixed price, or a retainer against a scope we write down each month. I don't bill by the hour as the work goes.
- Every proposal has two options: the smallest honest version that does the job, and the whole scope. A lower price takes a named piece of the scope with it.
- A workshop is a price for the day. Travel is one flat line beside it. Coaching is a fixed block of hours, never an open count.

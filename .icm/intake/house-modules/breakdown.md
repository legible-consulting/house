# Breakdown: The modules the house does not have yet

- epic-slug: house-modules
- source: the goal interview of 2026-10-14 (`library/research/design-trail/01-goals.md`, one goal per module) and the flattening of the house on 2026-10-25, which turned seven stub contracts into these stubs

## What I understood

The goal interview named sixteen modules. Seven of them exist: the core, the client folder, the desk, prospecting, the proposal, the quote, the build, conformance and research. Seven do not: client research, the agreement, kickoff, handover, the portfolio, content and training. Each had a folder with a stub contract that promised a process and held none. The folders left on 2026-10-25; each module is one stub here until a real deal or a real workshop walks it and a session writes its folder and contract from that walk. The desk's contract exists with its process unwritten; that is the eighth stub.

## Build order

1. `client-research`: before the first call with a prospect.
2. `agreement`: at the first deal that reaches signature.
3. `kickoff`: at the first signed build.
4. `handover`: at the first release.
5. `desk-process`: from the running desk, any time.
6. `portfolio`: at the first finished build, from the four cases on jamienisbet.com.
7. `content`: after the portfolio.
8. `training`: at the first workshop booked.

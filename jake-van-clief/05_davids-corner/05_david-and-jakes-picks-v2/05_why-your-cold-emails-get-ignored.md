# Why your cold emails get ignored

*David's Corner › David and Jake's Picks V2 · Author: Muhammad (member; post summarised by David's Corner) · Source: text lesson (links to original post)*

## Summary

Muhammad's case links website research, extraction and outreach drafting with n8n and OpenAI; his post title claims about nine booked calls a week. The page treats those figures as his case only. The exercise: take a fictional business, pick a single checkable fact about it and keep where it came from, then write one relevant opening sentence that is never sent. The review asks whether the line reflects the source, explains relevance and avoids invented familiarity, and says to follow applicable site and communication rules for real outreach and to measure your own results.

## Core concepts

- **One verifiable detail, with a saved source.**
- **Relevance stated, familiarity not faked.**
- **Draft stays unsent** until reviewed.
- **Own results first:** measure before scaling.

## Claims and evidence

- Nine calls a week — in the post title, self-reported anecdote — not verified.

## Apply to icm-workshop

- **S1 prospecting:** adapt for outreach drafts in the prospect's language: each draft cites one sourced detail from the target list; salesmen see sources next to the draft on the dashboard.
- Boundary: drafts only; sending to anyone is never done by a session.
- Reject n8n or an automated sending chain; it would be an orchestrator and a sending path.
- Check local rules on cold outreach (Portugal and EU contexts) before any real send.

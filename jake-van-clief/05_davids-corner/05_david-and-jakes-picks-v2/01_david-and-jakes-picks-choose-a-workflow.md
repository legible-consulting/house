# David and Jake's Picks: Choose a Workflow

*David's Corner › David and Jake's Picks V2 · Author: David's Corner (author not stated) · Source: text lesson*

## Summary

The module's index page. It groups eight member examples by the problem you have: continuing between sessions, getting a clearer build, understanding client work, or operating a repeated workflow. Each example links to the original member post and discussion. The advice is to pick one, write down input, result, tools and review step, and test on fictional data with a copy of any files you change. Member-reported savings, reply rates and client wins are treated as their cases, to be judged against your own baseline.

## Core concepts

- **Problem-first routing** to examples instead of a reading order.
- **Reusable core:** the task structure, sources, saved work and checks, not the specific assistant.
- **Own baseline:** a member's numbers do not transfer.

## Apply to icm-workshop

- Weak direct relevance as an index. Its grouping is a useful lens on the other eight notes in this module: session state, planning, outreach and client discovery are most relevant to C2, S1 and S2.
- Reuse the "fictional data, copy of files" rule for O4 workshop exercises.

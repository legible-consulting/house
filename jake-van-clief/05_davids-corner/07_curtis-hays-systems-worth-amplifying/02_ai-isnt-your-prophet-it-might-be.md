# AI Isn’t Your Prophet. It Might Be Your Amplifier.

*David's Corner › Curtis Hays - Systems Worth Amplifying · Author: Curtis Hays · Source: text lesson (essay dated 2 Mar 2026)*

## Summary

Curtis argues that generative AI lets a team produce a full marketing campaign in under an hour, but speed only magnifies whatever system sits underneath. If the company misunderstands its customer, or its website, ads and sales deck tell different stories, AI just scales that incoherence with better polish. He offers a six-layer foundation to build before leaning on AI, and a list of what AI does well once the foundation exists versus what it cannot do. The takeaway: ask whether you have a system worth amplifying, not whether to use AI.

## Core concepts

- **AI as amplifier, not prophet:** the tool is indifferent to quality. Coherent inputs compound into growth; incoherent ones compound into waste.
- **Mirror marketing:** messaging that talks about the company (who we are, what our product does) and points inward. AI polishes the mirror but cannot turn it toward the customer's world.
- **Statistical average problem:** models generate from the average of a category, so output is interchangeable with competitors unless specific customer truth and a distinctive story are supplied.
- **Chaos Tax:** the gap between what a lead costs and what a lead that becomes revenue costs. It never appears on a P&L, compounds each quarter, and gets worse when AI scales misaligned work.
- **Slope, not cliff:** drift between marketing and sales builds gradually, and dashboards can look fine the whole time.

## Frameworks and methods

The argument, in order:

1. AI makes polished output nearly free, so output quality stops being a signal of effectiveness.
2. Without an aligned base, that output runs the existing misalignment faster and cheaper-looking.
3. Most firms market inward; the lever that lowers cost without more spend is looking outward at the customer's tension and language.
4. The right foundation is built in a fixed order, each layer protecting the next: psychology, data, story, community, owned audience, measurement.
5. With that foundation in place, AI can enforce voice, resist drift, speed research synthesis, optimise campaigns and personalise by psychological cluster.
6. Some things remain human: feeling identity tension, knowing what a customer fears losing, building belonging, earning trust through vulnerability.
7. Conclusion: the foundation is the whole conversation, and diagnosis comes before deployment.

## Claims and evidence

- Content can be produced in about 45 minutes but will not work without alignment — author assertion from client work, no data shown.
- Most firms do not know the cost of a lead that becomes revenue — anecdotal observation from conversations with clients.
- AI amplifies wrong definitions, for example a mistaken MQL definition leads to scoring the wrong leads faster — reasoned example, not tested.
- The ordering of the six layers is presented as a doctrine; no evidence that this exact order is required.

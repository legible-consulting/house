# AI Acronym Overload? Here's the Cheat Sheet

*David's Corner › Must Have Resources · Author: David · Source: text lesson*

## Summary

A starter glossary David built after community threads became hard to follow. It groups common abbreviations into four clusters: core AI and learning, model types and architectures, prompting/retrieval/training methods, and practical business terms. The page stresses it is a lookup aid, not a curriculum, and that the real test is whether you can use a term in a sentence from a lesson and say what it changes in that task.

## Core concepts

- **Glossary as lookup, not syllabus:** decode the term, then return to the work.
- **Retrieval versus training:** RAG supplies material at answer time and does not retrain the model; LoRA-style methods change a small set of trained parameters. The page uses this contrast as its worked example.
- **Written reasoning is not proof:** the chain-of-thought entry carries a caveat that an explanation does not establish correctness.
- **Ambiguity rule:** if a label could mean two things, ask the author which they meant.

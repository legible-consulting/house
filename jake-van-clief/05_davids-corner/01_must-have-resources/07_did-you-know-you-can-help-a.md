# Did you know you can help a friend and make $$$

*David's Corner › Must Have Resources · Author: David's Corner (author not stated) · Source: text lesson*

## Summary

An announcement of the community's affiliate programme: members earn a recurring 20% commission on referred subscriptions. The page states example monthly payouts for the two paid tiers, a 14-day pending window tied to refunds, a 14-day referral cookie, and payout through a bank route. It also warns that plan changes, refunds and leave-and-rejoin can reduce a commission and tells readers to audit the dashboard rather than assume. The pitch rests on recommending only if you would tell a friend anyway.

## Core concepts

- **Recurring referral share:** commission continues while the referee stays subscribed.
- **Pending then confirmed:** earnings sit pending through the refund window.
- **Audit rather than assume:** commission can be adjusted after plan changes or refunds.
- **Recommendation integrity:** share the link only for something you would endorse unprompted.

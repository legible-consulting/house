# Professional AI-Assisted Business Writing

*David's Corner › Reviews - Tools, Models, Methods · Author: David's Corner (author not stated; inspired by member Greg Prince) · Source: text lesson (attachment: business-comm rules)*

## Summary

A checklist for using an assistant on emails, reports, proposals and team messages while the sender stays accountable for facts, tone and commitments. It recommends a brief with audience, purpose, verified facts, desired response, length and a voice sample, and an instruction not to invent names, figures, promises or sources, with a list of things to verify returned. Review runs in four passes: facts, purpose, voice, and recipient context. Practice is a short project update that must keep done, open and proposed distinct.

## Core concepts

- **Brief with a voice sample** and explicit missing-facts flag.
- **Four review passes:** facts, purpose, voice, recipient and channel.
- **Sender owns commitments.**
- **Permitted material only** goes into the tool.

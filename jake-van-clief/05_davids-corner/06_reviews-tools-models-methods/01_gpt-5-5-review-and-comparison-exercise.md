*David's Corner › Reviews - Tools, Models, Methods · Author: David's Corner (author not stated) · Source: text lesson*

## Summary

A dated review of GPT-5.5 (April 2026 release) rewritten around a reusable question: does a model improve your own task enough to justify changing the workflow? The page points to the vendor's model page and guidance for inputs, tools, limits and pricing, and supplies a comparison method: a small task with non-sensitive sources and three acceptance criteria, identical inputs and tools for each model, recorded model, date and settings, inspected output, then a second example. Original benchmark figures are retained only as dated reference.

## Core concepts

- **Same inputs, same tools** for every model compared.
- **Record version, date and settings** with each run.
- **Whole-run cost:** lower token use does not offset a higher price automatically; subscriptions and API billing differ.
- **Review effort counts** in the verdict.
- **Self-checking is not correctness.**

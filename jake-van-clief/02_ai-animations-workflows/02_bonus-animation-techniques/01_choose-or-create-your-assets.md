# 2.1 Choose or Create Your Assets

*AI Animations & Workflows › Module 2: Bonus Animation Techniques · Author: Jake Van Clief · Source: text lesson*

## Summary

A short text lesson on where visual assets come from: draw them in code, make your own artwork, or use a properly licensed asset. For simple diagrams, one small consistent SVG icon set is usually enough. The advice is to make one asset serve the explanation before building a library, and to verify usage terms before bundling anything into a shared download.

## Core concepts

- **Start from what the viewer must recognise:** a folder, person, connection or change, not from an asset catalogue.
- **Consistency over variety:** pick a handful of icons sharing a visual style.
- **Licence hygiene:** keep required notices with redistributed sources and check the original asset page for terms before putting anything in a shared package.
- **One asset, one purposeful animation:** import, animate one change, pause on the busiest frame and check legibility at the intended size.

## Tools, settings and resources

# Animation Workflow: Quick Reference

*AI Animations & Workflows › Module 2: Bonus Animation Techniques · Author: Jake Van Clief · Source: text lesson*

## Summary

A one-page reference that restates the five-stage narrated workflow as input-to-output handoffs with a human check at each, plus a rule for which stage to revisit when something is wrong. It also collects links for the course, the Premium package, the earlier Remotion route and background lessons. It is a reminder sheet, not a new method.

## Core concepts

- **Each stage turns one reviewed artefact into the next:** reviewed script to approved audio; audio to checked timed transcript; timed phrases to reviewed storyboard; cues and assets to a working animation; scene and audio to a playable file.
- **Fix the stage that owns the problem:** wording or voice changes force re-approval of audio and timing; emphasis or order is a cue problem; covered text or a late visual is a scene problem; export errors get debugged from the real error with a short render test.
- **Separate sources, instructions and outputs:** the demonstrated workspace numbers its stage folders in pipeline order; a self-built one may name them differently, so read its own guide.
- **Start small:** audience, one idea and a small result before anything else.

## Frameworks and methods

The five stages are voice, words, cues, scene, render. The ownership rule above is a lightweight dependency map: downstream artefacts become stale when an upstream one changes.

# 1.8 Make the Next Video with Your Own Idea

*AI Animations & Workflows › Module 1: Your First Animation · Author: Jake Van Clief · Source: Skool video · Length: ~2:20*

## Summary

Closing exercise for Module 1: take a small idea from real work, such as a step clients keep asking about, and run it through the same five stages in a new, separate project, leaving the practice project intact. The AI follows the pipeline and brings each output to the learner at the review points. Preferences that worked are saved as reusable instructions so the next project starts with the same expectations.

## Core concepts

- **New project, same tooling:** reuse stage instructions and drawing tools, supply new facts, audience and a single outcome; start at roughly thirty seconds and one idea.
- **Briefing flags unsupported claims:** the AI drafts the brief and must flag anything the source does not support, before any scripting.
- **Decision points are named up front:** the learner says which decision they want to make before the AI continues, and saves that rule for next time.
- **Dependency discipline:** changing narration means a new voice take and re-checked timings; changing only visual style can keep the reviewed voice but the new look must still teach the same thing.
- **Carry preferences forward:** shorter sentences, bigger source text, keeping the original visible during comparisons become reusable instructions.
- **External test:** show the export to someone unfamiliar and ask what they understood; use that for the next correction.

## Frameworks and methods

Handoff review checklist in prose: brief and script match the source; voice and timings are checked against the chosen take; each visual explains its phrase; the actual exported file is watched and tested on an outsider. Share the video, the idea, tools used and one post-review change.

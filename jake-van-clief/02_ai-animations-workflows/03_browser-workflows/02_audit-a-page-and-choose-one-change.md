# 3.2 Audit a Page and Choose One Change

*AI Animations & Workflows › Module 3: Browser Workflows · Author: Jake Van Clief · Source: text lesson*

## Summary

Before editing any web page, inventory what is on it and pick one small change worth making. The lesson uses a lesson page in an online classroom as the example, where body text, player, attachments and discussion live in different controls, so a title-only capture proves little. The output is a short inventory plus one reviewable proposal with a preservation list and a plan for checking the saved result.

## Core concepts

- **Inventory what was actually inspected:** URL, title, publication state, teaching, links and their destinations, media, resources, discussion; anything not inspected stays marked unknown.
- **One concrete change:** an unclear next step, outdated instruction or a link pointing to the wrong lesson, stated as current text, replacement and reason.
- **Stop after the proposal:** the audit run does not change anything.
- **Preserve evidence:** save the original body and screenshots of media or resources, because copying text does not preserve a player or discussion. With read-only access, prepare the replacement locally and label it a draft.
- **Links:** compare destinations as well as labels; a changed tracking parameter is a different thing from a changed destination.

## Frameworks and methods

Audit, then propose, then list what must stay and how to check the result, then pick a pilot small enough to inspect afterwards.

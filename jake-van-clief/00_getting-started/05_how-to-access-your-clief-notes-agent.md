# How to Access your Clief Notes Agent & Roadmap

*Getting Started · Author: Jose Guerra (Decster co-founder) · Source: linked video (Decster walkthrough) · Length: ~6:30 · Link: https://www.loom.com/share/ed73777dabf9471f886f69db8dbcf619*

## Summary

A third-party walkthrough of Decster, a service that layers a Chrome-extension chat agent and a step-by-step roadmap over the Skool classroom. You register with your Skool email, answer a profile questionnaire, install the extension, and then the agent recommends lessons, personalises how a lesson applies to you, and tracks roadmap tasks (post, quiz, continue). The text page adds the registration link and a mobile route.

## Tools, settings and resources

- **Decster student portal and Chrome extension** — onboarding profile, in-page chat agent, roadmap with tasks and quizzes — sign up with the same email as Skool; mobile has a separate walkthrough.

## Claims and evidence

# 🚀 Levels Explained

*Getting Started · Author: Jake Van Clief · Source: text lesson*

## Summary

Explains the community's gamified level ladder: members gain points when others like their posts and comments, titles change automatically, and higher levels unlock rewards. The Foundation is never locked; complementary builds unlock through activity or payment.

## Core concepts

- **Points come from likes** on your posts, comments and replies, per Skool's standard mechanism; the leaderboard shows standing.
- **Nine levels with document-themed names** run from Footnote through Index, Compiler and Architect to Orchestrator; the names mirror the community's subject (documents, indexes, compiled work, systems).
- **Rewards sit at the top:** free lifetime access to the paid Vault at level 8, a cash reward at level 9.
- **Faster levelling** comes from thoughtful questions, shared progress and helping others.

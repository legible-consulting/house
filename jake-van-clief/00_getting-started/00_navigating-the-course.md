# 📚Navigating The Course

*Getting Started · Author: Jake Van Clief · Source: Skool video + text page · Length: ~3:00 (estimated from captions)*

## Summary

An orientation to the Clief Notes community: where posts, classroom, leaderboard and member map live, how points and levels gate posting, and how lesson completion ticks work. The video is mostly about community mechanics and incentives; the text page beside it is a short router that sends newcomers to the Foundation start page, the animations course and the "choose your next course" page, and tells them how to ask for help well.

## Core concepts

- **Posting is level-gated:** new members can read but cannot post until they reach level 2; after that they can attach files, links, videos and polls, and tag posts into categories (resources, wins, troubleshooting, show-your-work).
- **Levels double as titles:** each level carries a badge, the leaderboard shows points needed, and the Getting Started room lists the unlocks (a cash reward at the top level, lifetime paid-content access at level 8).
- **Progress ticks matter:** the completion checkmark in each lesson drives the learner's percentage-done, and Jake asks people to tick them.
- **Lesson check-ins and comments:** each lesson ends with a vote-style check-in and a comment thread he says he tries to answer.
- **Foundations is framed as durable:** he positions it as knowledge that stays useful for years, as opposed to the faster-moving paid builds.

## Tools, settings and resources

- **Skool Classroom / Leaderboard / Calendar / Map tabs** — navigation, points, live sessions, member geography (approximate locations only).
- **Help-request habit (page)** — name the lesson, what you tried and the exact error; keep client data and credentials out of public posts.

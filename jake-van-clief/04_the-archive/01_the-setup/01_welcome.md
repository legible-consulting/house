## Summary

This is the front door to the Archive course: a short chooser page, not teaching content. It tells the reader that the Archive holds the ideas behind the method (essays and older recordings), and that anyone wanting a working setup should go to Foundation 1.4 "Pick Your Setup" instead. It sorts the nine lessons by the question you bring (abstraction, learning and work, ownership, orchestration) and suggests either reading in order or picking one question and leaving with a single decision you can explain and check.

## Core concepts

- **Archive versus Foundation:** the Archive is history and argument; Foundation is the practical build path. The page explicitly separates "read for the why" from "set up for the how".
- **Question-led routing:** the lessons are grouped by intent rather than strictly by order, so a reader can jump to the one that answers their current question.
- **Context stays in its era:** dates, product names, forecasts and first-person claims are left in their original context, and research claims come with scope notes beside them.
- **Each lesson has a pattern:** a short guide, an exercise, then the original essay or video companion.

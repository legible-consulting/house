# WELCOME TO THE WISE ARCHITECTS CORNER

*THE LEGENDS › 1. Bas Rosario – Wise Architects Library · Author: Bas Rosario · Source: text lesson*

## Summary

Bas introduces himself as a member and moderator of the community who leads IT teams by day, and explains how his corner is organised. Every series follows an identical four-part rhythm, so a learner always knows where they stand and what comes next. The stated aim is not to hand over answers but to build the understanding that makes the answers the learner's own. It is orientation only, with no technique content, and points to the Prompt Engineering overview as the first stop.

## Core concepts

- **Fixed series rhythm:** three connected classes, then a payoff class where the three ideas combine, then a quick card, then a personal perspective piece. Learning is framed as sticking because of this predictability.
- **No prerequisites:** no programming background is needed; curiosity is the entry requirement.
- **Orientation as a promise:** Bas borrows a leadership habit, always showing where the team stands, and applies it to teaching so no learner wonders what is next.
- **Understanding over answers:** the series are designed to build mental models, not hand out ready-made prompts.

## Frameworks and methods

The four-beat series structure (classes, payoff, card, perspective) is the only method here. It is worth noticing as a design pattern in its own right: a fixed shape that every series repeats.

## Apply to icm-workshop

- **Adapt** the repeat-the-same-shape idea for O4 course material: every workshop module could share one skeleton (concept, practice, reference card, reflection), which also supports the one-home-per-fact rule because the skeleton lives once in the shared core (C1).

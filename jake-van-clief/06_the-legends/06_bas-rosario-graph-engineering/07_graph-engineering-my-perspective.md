# GRAPH ENGINEERING 🔍 My perspective

*THE LEGENDS › 6. Bas Rosario - Graph Engineering · Author: Bas Rosario · Source: text lesson*

## Summary

Bas gives his working view of rung five. He leads teams in his day job, so graph concepts looked like old management practice under new vocabulary: brief, handover, review. He runs "councils": bounded workers doing narrow tasks with a separate judge, bound by a few structural rules. He gives a video-build example, then states his sharpest rule: stay in one room until there is a reason to leave. He ends with how he nests scopes in his working life and closes the ladder by saying the load-bearing part is always written by a person.

## Core concepts

- **People and machines both lack shared context:** the discipline for managing the first turns out to manage the second.
- **Four structural rules for councils:** no two workers write to the same place; contracts are frozen before anyone runs; the producer is never the one who blesses the output; a worker that hits a wall stops and asks upward instead of guessing.
- **Agreement without contact is evidence:** independent workers converging on the same answer cannot be an echo.
- **Failures are mostly structural, not intelligence failures.**
- **Do not fan out by default:** three valid reasons: pieces are truly independent, work outgrew one worker's attention, or pieces would overwrite each other. Otherwise one well-briefed worker beats several poorly briefed ones.
- **Nested scopes:** a top level holds what is true everywhere and routes into projects, which route into stages; each layer holds only its own scope and passes a clean bounded brief downward.

## Claims and evidence

- A three-worker parallel video build (sourcing, quality gate, authoring) with an independent reviewer worked cleanly — personal anecdote.
- Working life organised as nested scopes — self-reported.

## Apply to icm-workshop

- **Folder tree is the orchestration, confirmed.** His nested-scope description (global, project, stage, each holding only its own world) is essentially the layered ICM structure Jamie already uses, and it supports one home per fact and every module starting cold.
- **Adopt** "producer is never the judge" for gates: the session that drafts a proposal or quote (S3/S4) does not bless it; Jamie does.
- **Adopt** "stop and ask up the chain" as the rule for unattended runs: the three allowed jobs should hand back a flagged line for the morning glance (C3) rather than guess.
- **Reject** the council as a standing architecture; use it at most as an in-stage technique. His default of staying in one room also fits Jamie's solo scale and the "never build an orchestrator" boundary.

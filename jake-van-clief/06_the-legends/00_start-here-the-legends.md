# START HERE – THE LEGENDS

*THE LEGENDS › (course landing page) · Author: Clief Notes community (trail map covering Bas Rosario and Don Roy) · Source: text lesson*

## Summary

A signpost page for the classroom that gathers two contributor corners: Bas Rosario, who teaches how the underlying system works, and Don Roy, who shares practical build and quality workflows. It does not teach a technique itself. Its value is the routing: depending on whether you are starting from zero, wanting the architecture series, or wanting to put a chat habit into a folder, it points you to a specific lesson order. It closes by asking for one small result plus one correction posted back to the community.

## Core concepts

- **Two corners, two jobs:** Bas is about understanding (prompt, context, memory and later loop and graph thinking); Don is about building and checking things in practice.
- **Start from one small job:** the entry exercise is to pick a single modest task, name the source material you will hand over, and name the result you will inspect. The pattern is "source in, inspectable result out".
- **Foundation as the on-ramp:** beginners are sent back to the Foundation lessons on setup, chat and folders-plus-one-agent before the Bas series.
- **The ladder is only partly published:** the page notes that prompt and context are the complete series in this route, while memory, loop and graph are introduced as rungs but should be followed through linked practice rather than searched for as missing classes. (Other folders in this repo hold the later series that do exist; check their notes rather than trusting this line.)
- **Share responsibly:** the closing task says to post only material you have permission to share.

## Frameworks and methods

1. Pick one small job and write down its input and expected output.
2. Take the matching Bas series: prompt (name the task, choose its shape, diagnose weak answers) or context (choose source material, examples, boundaries and finish line).
3. Use the "where this fits in ICM" lessons to turn a chat habit into an inspectable workspace.
4. Reuse the same pattern for a second job via the Foundation lessons on folders and handing work on.

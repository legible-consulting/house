## Summary

Bas maps the durable-versus-disposable split from class one onto two layers of the ICM conventions, reference material and working artifacts. He ties class two's drawer to the "loaded selectively" label and the one-fact-one-place rule to the conventions' wording about a single home per fact. The notable finding is what ICM does not provide: he reports searching the paper and conventions for a date and finding none, so nothing on the shelf marks a line as stale, and nothing promotes repeated fixes upward by itself. Dating, review and promotion therefore remain the author's own job.

## Core concepts

- **Layer 3, the shelf:** reference material, stable across runs, answering "what rules apply". Layer 4 is the workbench of artefacts that change every run. In the conventions' phrasing, "Layer 3 is the factory. Layer 4 is the product."
- **Selective loading is the drawer:** both layers are loaded selectively, matching class two's small always-loaded part plus pointers to the rest.
- **One home per fact:** other files point to the home and do not duplicate it.
- **Edit the source, not the output:** correcting the source file fixes future runs; correcting only the output fixes one run. The page says this appears in the paper's future-directions discussion, not as present behaviour.
- **No staleness mechanism:** a reference file stays until someone removes it; lines carry no since-when or says-who unless you add them; promotion from workbench to shelf is only a possible future feature.
- **The next rung is visible:** the Outputs line of a stage and who checks it lead into the loop series.

## Apply to icm-workshop

- **Adopt** the Layer 3 versus Layer 4 distinction for the shared core and client folders: C1 files are the stable shelf read selectively; C2 records and module artefacts are the changing workbench.
- **Adapt** by adding what ICM lacks: every fact line in C1 and C2 carries a since-when and a source, and the goals file's rule that every rule carries its own why and date should be extended to facts. D4 conformance can then flag undated lines.
- **Adopt** "edit the source, not the output" as a workflow rule: corrections to a proposal or quote go back into the C1 or C2 source file, so every future run benefits.
- **Reject** relying on ICM alone for freshness; plan the review sweep explicitly.

# Sunday Coffee ☕️

*THE LEGENDS › Don Roy – Master Builder · Author: Don Roy · Source: text lesson*

## Summary

A recurring weekly discussion thread with a fixed set of prompts (current work, something to ship, blockers, where help is wanted, anything else), plus an occasional poll. The page addendum suggests adding one inspectable item and the smallest next action, then reporting what happened at the next check-in.

## Apply to icm-workshop

- **C3 / O4:** the "state the next action, then report on it next week" shape matches the Friday review (next action and deadline per client); adapt it for coaching records.

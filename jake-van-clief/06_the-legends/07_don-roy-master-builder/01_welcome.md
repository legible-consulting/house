# Welcome! 🤝

*THE LEGENDS › Don Roy – Master Builder · Author: Don Roy · Source: text lesson*

## Summary

Don introduces his corner of the classroom. He is a mechanical engineer who learns this field on the side, started with limited tokens and no coding background, and presents his section as a learning journey rather than expert teaching. He lists what he shares: short videos, thoughts, example builds, deep dives and essays. A page addendum routes newcomers to the Foundation live lesson and the small-workspace exercise first, then to the sub-areas of his section by need (question, practice resource, weekly check-in, video).

## Core concepts

- **Learner-first stance:** Don says he is anywhere from two to twenty steps from any given reader and learns from the community.
- **Constraint as context:** limited tokens and no coding experience shape his builds towards simple, low-cost approaches.
- **Section map:** Back to Basics for questions, Resources for practice material, Sunday Coffee for weekly check-ins, Vlog for videos.

# Where to Go Next

*The Foundation › Where to Go Next · Author: Jake Van Clief · Source: text lesson*

## Summary

The closing signpost for the Foundation course. It assumes the learner has seen the three layers, how to build around an outcome, and where to automate versus keep judgment, then asks them to pick one real job and follow a single route. Routes cover chat, skills and projects, folders with one agent, tool choice, building around a real job, and turning steady steps into code, with further sections for animation and browser examples, team and deeper material, community mentors, paid Premium packages and a VIP help tier. It adds no new concepts; it is a menu with a bias toward finishing one useful result.

## Core concepts

- **Pick one job, finish one result:** the page's organising idea is outcome-first routing rather than completing the whole library.
- **Start with a folder that does one job well:** extra agents or complexity should be earned, and the team-oriented lessons are framed around when another agent justifies itself, keeping stages inspectable and handing over a copy.
- **Match route to need:** keep weekly corrections (skills and projects), work inside files (folders and one agent), choose tools, or automate a steady step as code.
- **Check before opening up access:** the remote-access example is flagged with a reminder to confirm setup and permissions first.

## Frameworks and methods

1. Six "finish with one useful result" routes: chat, skills and projects, folders and one agent, pick your setup, outcome then folder design, steady parts into code.
2. Escalation path for teams: one model with different jobs, then inspectable stages, then handing it on and working in one shared place.
